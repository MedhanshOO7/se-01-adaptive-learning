# Fractions workshop

Choose the explanation for your weakest topic, then try the questions below.

## Equal parts

Four equal pieces make quarters. Unequal pieces cannot each be called one quarter of the same whole.

## Equivalence

Multiply both terms by the same number: 1/2 = 2/4 = 3/6.

## Comparison

Give fractions the same denominator. Then compare their numerators: 1/4 < 2/4 < 3/4.

## Simplifying

Divide both terms by their greatest common factor. 6/8 becomes 3/4.

## Addition

For equal denominators, add numerators: 1/4 + 2/4 = 3/4.

## Reasoning

For the same whole, more equal pieces means smaller individual pieces. One third is smaller than one half.
