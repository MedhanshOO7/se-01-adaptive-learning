# Make sense of fractions

A whole, split into equal parts. A small idea that opens up a lot.

## Start with the same whole

In 3/4, the whole is divided into four equal pieces. Three of them are yours. The pieces must be equal — and when you compare fractions, the wholes must be the same size.

Multiplying both terms gives an equivalent fraction: 1/2 and 2/4 name the same amount. A common denominator helps you compare fractions and add them together.
