## Scope and interpretation

The source is the **new** Grade 9 chapter 8, not the older Grade 9 chapter 4 and not Class 12 Physics. Page numbers below refer to the printed book. Content in these notes is paraphrased, with original examples and questions clearly distinguished from textbook references. It is a research package, not an exhaustive verbatim copy, and textbook diagrams and individual exercise wording are not reproduced. Check the source before using its figures or text in a published product; availability of a PDF alone does not establish reproduction rights.

The book sometimes simplifies models for school use. In particular, Bohr shells are a historical model, not literal electron tracks; average atomic mass must not be confused with an integer mass number. The chapter's isotope and valency simplifications should be taught with their limits. Any learning-depth levels and prerequisite edges in this folder are **proposed authoring judgments**, not official NCERT or CBSE classifications.
