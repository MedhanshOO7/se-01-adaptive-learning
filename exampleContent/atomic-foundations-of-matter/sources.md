## Boundaries

The 2026 syllabus includes bonding and properties as well as formulas and masses. Historical Dalton statements must be distinguished from modern exceptions. Proposed depth levels, links, questions and remediation below are authoring judgments, not official board claims. The mole, Avogadro constant and molar mass are not listed among this CBSE topic's key concepts; do not assume they are required from an older syllabus. Actual exam scope depends on official and school instructions.
